Formulas: LTV = revenue per account x gross margin / monthly churn (the simple form, constant churn; with 100% margin, $50 and 4% churn gives $1,250, as in the Subscription Index guide). Lifetime = 1 / churn. CAC payback = CAC / (revenue x gross margin) in months. Annual churn = 1 - (1 - monthly)^12. Rules of thumb used for the verdict, widely quoted and not laws: LTV:CAC of about 3:1 or better, above 5:1 may mean under-investing in growth, payback within about 12 months (Wall Street Prep, Prospeo, CVF). Simple LTV is optimistic when churn is early-heavy or you have little history: check it against real cohorts. Use revenue churn if you can. Runway = cash / net monthly burn (costs minus revenue); with growth, revenue compounds monthly and costs stay flat, which is a simplification. Runway is a plan, not a forecast.
